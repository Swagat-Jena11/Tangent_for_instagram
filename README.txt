Tangent for Instagram
=====================
An installable phone app (PWA). Your Instagram export is read on the phone only.

Put it on your phone (free, about 5 minutes)
1. On github.com, create a new public repository, e.g. "tangent".
2. Upload every file in this folder (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, icon-192.png, icon-512.png).
3. Repository Settings -> Pages -> Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder: / (root). Save.
4. After a minute, open https://<your-username>.github.io/tangent/ on your phone.
5. Android Chrome: menu -> "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
   iPhone Safari: Share -> "Add to Home Screen".

It then opens full screen from its own icon and works offline.
