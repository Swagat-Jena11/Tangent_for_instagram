Bring in your Instagram

Your export is read on this phone only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Request your export
  1. In Instagram, open Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information.
  2. Tap Create export, pick your profile, then Export to device.
  3. Under Customize information, keep Your Instagram activity, Connections, Preferences and Ads information. Tick Messages if you want your chats. Untick media to keep the file small.
  4. Set Format: JSON and Date range: All time, then start the export.
  5. When Instagram notifies you, download the ZIP to this phone.
